Our Story

Built on a Simple Belief — Healthy Birds, Healthy People

PARAH INTEGRATED FARM'S was founded with one purpose: to raise quality poultry the right way. We believe that what goes into a bird matters — clean water, balanced feed, proper vaccination, and enough space to grow well.

What started as a small backyard farm has grown into a trusted supplier of fresh eggs, live chickens (any breed) and Fish, Rabbit, Somked fish & chicken (well packed and Ganished), Pig, Cow, Duck, Turkey and organic manure for families, restaurants, and fellow farmers across our region.

We don't cut corners. Every crate of eggs and every bird that leaves our farm meets the standard we set for our own family's table.
